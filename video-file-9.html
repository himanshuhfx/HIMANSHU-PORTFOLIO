Transit: Ghar Se Kaam II

A rhythmic study of daily life in Jaipur. Through the lens of an auto-rickshaw and public buses, this edit uses text-tracking and ambient sound design to turn a routine commute into a visual story about the city's movement.

Technical Specs: Shot on iPhone 15; Post-production in After Effects (3D Text Tracking, Color Matching).
